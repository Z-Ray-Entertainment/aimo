# 🏰 The Hub Dashboard

[← Back to Overview](README.md) | [← Initial Setup](first_boot.md) | [Player Personas →](personas.md)

Following a successful login, you land directly on the main central hub of AImo. From here, you have immediate access to all roleplay characters, lorebooks, and world settings.

---

## 👤 Header Navigation & User Menu

In the top-right corner of the header bar, you find a circular button displaying the portrait image of your current default persona (or a monochrome user icon if no persona is selected yet). Clicking this icon opens your personal account menu:
- **Creator-Tag:** Your Creator-Tag is shown in **Settings → Account**, where you can copy it with one click. Share it privately with trusted authors so they can add you as a collaborator.
- **Einstellungen (`⚙️ Einstellungen`):** Available to all logged-in users; opens your personal [User Settings](settings.md) with your [Personas](personas.md).
- **Admin-Einstellungen (`⚙️ Admin-Einstellungen`):** Visible to administrators; opens the [Administration & Settings](admin.md) area.
- **Logout (`🚪 Logout`):** Located below a visual divider line to end your current session.
- **Support & Website Links:** At the very bottom of the menu below another divider line, two icon buttons link in a new tab to Ko-Fi (*Support us on ko-fi*) and the Z-Ray Entertainment website (*Visit our Website*, automatically opening the German or English edition based on your selected language).

On smaller or mobile screens, the three main Hub navigation sections (*KI-Charaktere*, *Lorebooks*, *Worlds*) also appear at the top of this menu above a divider line.

---

## 🗂️ The Three Main Tabs

Beneath the header, navigation is split across three thematic tabs with monochrome icons:

### 1. Tab: Characters (`👤`)
The central gallery of all AI characters accessible to you (public characters created across the system as well as your own private characters):
- **New Character (First Tile):**  
  If your account has permission to create characters, the very first tile is a placeholder with a green plus icon (`+`). Clicking it opens the [Character Designer](characters.md).
- **Character Tiles:**
  - **Primary Portrait:** Large portrait image of the character enclosed in a wooden frame.
  - **Scrolling Description Text:** The personality description smoothly scrolls as an overlay across the bottom area of the portrait.
  - **Speech Bubble Icon (Bottom-Left):** Displays the total number of messages you have exchanged with this character across all chats and forks.
  - **Relationship Status Bar (-100 to +100):**
    - Displays the highest affection score achieved across all your chats with this character.
    - Dynamic color and heart indicator: Dark red with broken heart (💔) at -100, bright pink with sparkling heart (💖) at +100.
- **Filters & Sorting:**
  - **Geschlecht:** Filter nach allen Geschlechtern oder gezielt nach *Weiblich* (`female`), *Männlich* (`male`) bzw. *Nicht-Binär* (`non-binary`).
  - **Chat-Status:** Filter nach allen Charakteren, bereits aktiven Chats (*Bereits gechattet*) oder noch unberührten Bekanntschaften (*Noch nicht gechattet*).
  - **Sortierung:** Sortierung nach *Neueste zuerst*, *Meiste Nachrichten* oder *Bester Beziehungswert*.
  - **Urheber:** Für Benutzer mit Ersteller-Berechtigung steht ganz rechts ein Filter bereit, um zwischen *Alle Charaktere*, *Nur eigene (Ersteller)* (ausschließlich selbst erstellte Figuren) und *Kollaborationen* (Charaktere befreundeter Autoren mit Mitwirkungsrechten) umzuschalten. Ohne Ersteller-Rechte wird dieses Auswahlfeld automatisch verborgen.
- **Clicking a Tile:** Directly launches or resumes a [Chat with this Character](chat.md).

### 2. Tab: Lorebooks (`📜`)
Overview of all thematic knowledge encyclopedias.
- **New Lorebook (`+`):** Opens the [Lorebook Editor](lorebooks.md) directly to create a new lorebook.
- **Tiles:** Show visibility (`Public` / `Private`), translation status badge if pending, title, and entry count. Hovering displays the lorebook description overlay. Clicking a tile opens the [Lorebook Editor](lorebooks.md).
- **Filters & Sorting:** Filter by **Visibility** (*All*, *Public*, *Private*), **Author** (*All Lorebooks*, *Only Mine*, *Collaborations* for creators), and **Sort Order** (*Newest First*, *Name (A-Z)*).

### 3. Tab: WorldInfo (`🌐`)
Overview of all world settings.
- **New World (`+`):** Opens the [WorldInfo Editor](worldinfo.md) directly to create a new world.
- **Tiles:** Display cover artwork (or world icon), visibility (`Public` / `Private`), title, and counts of included locations and events. Clicking a tile opens the [WorldInfo Editor](worldinfo.md).
- **Filters & Sorting:** Filter by **Visibility** (*All*, *Public*, *Private*), **Author** (*All Worlds*, *Only Mine*, *Collaborations* for creators), and **Sort Order** (*Newest First*, *Name (A-Z)*).

## 🔔 System Notifications & Floating Banderoles

System notifications, confirmations, and alerts appear as floating rectangular banderoles in the bottom-right corner of the screen:
- **Appearance:** New notices slide smoothly into view from the right side and stay parked in the lower-right area with comfortable spacing from the edge of the screen.
- **Color Stripe on the Left:** A vertical colored stripe on the left edge of each banderole immediately indicates the type of notification:
  - 🟢 **Green (Success):** Confirms that an action was successful — such as saving or updating a character, world, place, or lorebook.
  - 🟡 **Yellow (Warning):** Alerts you to timeouts, missing inputs, or temporary delays.
  - 🔴 **Red (Error):** Informs you when a connection problem occurred (e.g. if the server or AI service is unreachable) or an action failed.
  - 🟡/Gold **Gold (Info & Replies):** Shows general information or lets you know that a character has replied to you in another conversation.
- **Portraits & Action Buttons:** Character notifications can include the character's portrait image and a direct *„Zum Chat →“* button to jump straight into the dialogue.
- **Closing:** Banderoles close automatically after a few seconds, or you can dismiss them immediately by clicking the close button (`✕`) in the upper-right corner.

### Language Availability
- You see characters, worlds, and lorebooks that are already available in your selected account language.
- If additional content is still being translated into your language, an info notification informs you that the selection may still grow.
- Authors, collaborators, and administrators always see their own or editable creations—with a **Translation pending** badge if a translation into their current language has not yet been completed.
